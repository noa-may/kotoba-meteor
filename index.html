Ready to practice?

Tap the correct falling Japanese word before it reaches the ground. For kanji-reading questions, tapping a meteor reads the question kanji aloud. In other modes, tapping a meteor reads the selected word aloud. Incorrect taps or missed answers cost a life. Each session has 3 rounds of 10 questions. You can end the session at any time!

Every eligible word appears once per cycle before repeating.
